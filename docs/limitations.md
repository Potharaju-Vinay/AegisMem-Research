# Honest Research Limitations & Boundary Disclosures

To preserve scientific rigor, academic honesty, and defensible experimental claims, this document explicitly articulates the boundaries, assumptions, and constraints of our evaluation of AegisMem.

---

## 1. Domain & Scope Boundaries

* **Single Domain Instantiation:**
  - AegisMem is evaluated within the **Enterprise AI Customer Support** domain. While the core algorithms (TCRP, ReadPathGate, Replicated Trust Vault, Recovery Ladder) are domain-agnostic, our empirical benchmarks specifically evaluate customer support taxonomy, service level agreements, return policies, and consumer inquiries.
  - We do *not* claim that the framework "works in any arbitrary real-world environment" or across radically distinct agent architectures (e.g., embodied robotics or autonomous trading agents) without domain-specific dependency graph construction and policy modeling.

* **Realistic Testbed vs. Production Deployment:**
  - We deploy and benchmark AegisMem across a containerized multi-node testbed with segmented virtual networks, separate database credentials, an independent anchor witness container, and an external Kali attacker container.
  - This architecture represents a **realistic, high-fidelity security testbed**, not a live, battle-tested cloud production pilot. We explicitly avoid labeling the prototype as "production-ready enterprise software" until multi-month enterprise production pilots are conducted.

---

## 2. Dataset & Policy Synthetic Modeling

* **Synthetic Enterprise Policy Corpus:**
  - Proprietary enterprise policies, escalation thresholds, and internal SLA rulebooks are legally confidential and cannot be published or open-sourced.
  - Consequently, our 6-class memory hierarchy (`POLICY`, `PRICING_PROMOTIONS`, `ESCALATION_PROCEDURES`, `PRODUCT_FAQ`, `CUSTOMER_PROFILE`, `CONVERSATION_NOTE`) is modeled on standard consumer electronic, retail, and e-commerce return conventions.
  - All policy texts, FAQ items, and promotion rules were authored synthetically for this research. No proprietary corporate trade secrets or private data were utilized.

* **Public Query Datasets:**
  - Realistic user query distributions were derived from standard public conversational datasets: Bitext Customer Support Dataset (CDLA-Sharing-1.0) and Banking77 (CC BY 4.0).
  - Queries were matched against ground-truth policy bounds to evaluate factual precision and tamper resistance.

---

## 3. Cryptographic & Key Management Assumptions

* **Offline Staff Signing-Key Assumption:**
  - The security guarantees of owner-authorized updates rely on the signing-key assumption: enterprise administrators safeguard their private Ed25519 signing keys (`owner_private_key.pem`) on secure local administrative workstations or hardware security modules (HSMs).
  - If an administrative private key is stolen, an adversary can forge authenticated updates. AegisMem bounds this risk through the Key Revocation Registry (`core/security/revocation.py`) and single-use cryptographic nonces, but cannot prevent valid signatures generated by a currently unrevoked compromised key.

* **Anchor & Vault Credential Separation:**
  - The Recovery Ladder requires that credentials to the out-of-band append-only Trust Vault (`vault-db`) and independent Anchor Witness (`anchor`) are isolated from the primary live database credentials. If an attacker gains universal root access across all segmented network containers simultaneously, the system guarantees safety over availability by taking the fail-safe path (`ROOT_OF_TRUST_COMPROMISED`), halting automated responses.

---

## 4. LLM Non-Determinism & Baseline Framing

* **Stochastic LLM Mitigation:**
  - Generative language models inherently exhibit stochastic variability across inference runs.
  - To ensure scientific reproducibility, all evaluation experiments were conducted with temperature set to $0.0$, deterministic rule baselines were preserved as deterministic reference bounds, and statistical evaluations were conducted across 5 fixed random seeds ($N=5$) with paired statistical tests (McNemar's test and Wilcoxon signed-rank test).

* **Detection-Only Baseline ("CAMS-style" Paradigm):**
  - The CAMS (Context-Aware Memory Security) framework is closed-source and not publicly reproducible from published papers.
  - Our `detection_only` baseline represents the general *detect-and-stop* security paradigm from the academic literature (halting traffic upon detection without executing graph-cascaded self-healing recovery). It is not a literal line-by-line reproduction of proprietary CAMS code.

---

## 5. Latency Reporting Transparency

* **Recovery Latency Context:**
  - In our benchmarks, recovery latencies (e.g., $p_{50} = 18.47\text{ ms}$, recovery time $= 26.08\text{ ms} - 35.22\text{ ms}$) are measured from event-driven PostgreSQL notifications and SQLite reconciliation scans across 10, 50, and 100 concurrent customer sessions.
  - Recovery time measures database version restoration, cryptographic re-attestation, and derived state invalidation; it does not include client-side network round-trips over public wide-area networks (WAN).
